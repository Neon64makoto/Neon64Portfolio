Main Page:

Welcome to my website, this is my little portfolio.

On the right, there are buttons. About me will keep you on this website, but the other 3 will bring you to public Google Drive pages showing things I've created myself. On the bottom are my social links, the Discord button is my personal server. The Google Drive button brings you to the folder right before Coding, Models, and Creative Maps. Github will bring you to my profile. Settings will let you change the main color of the site from blue to red or green. Helpful if you are having trouble reading this. View Background will hide this main panel, click anywhere to show this panel again.

The video in the background is a Fortnite Creative map I made with UEFN, called Flatline City. It started out as just some new Japanese streets the Fortnite Devs had gracefully given me, but quickly unfurled into a full blown map. You can see more of it using the "Creative Maps" button. I also made a story for it after the fact, for a school project. I don't know when I decided that it was supposed to be dystopian, but when I was making most of it, that was not the idea, so to me it doesn't seem all that dystopian because it's too bright and colorful.

About Me:

I'm Makoto Bennett. I am (at the time of writing this) 17 years old, my birthday is 4/12/2009. I spent a good portion of my life making creative maps in Fortnite Creative, from Chapter 1 Season 7 when it came out, all the way to Chapter 7 Season 4, when I stopped using Fortnite all together.

Technically I've been coding since I was 10 or so because I started messing with Scratch. But when it comes to actually coding without the use of any AI, I have only recently started. Which is why this website has come to fruition. Before this I had a partially vibe coded website that I wasn't very happy with, and I knew I should make a website myself without the use of any AI. So that's what I did. And of course you are viewing that now.

I am currently still going through school, and when I graduate next year, I intend on going to a free coding bootcamp. My main passions are coding, helping with technology, and creating. I also love music, but I do not create any. Now that I don't use Fortnite Creative anymorem I have many options in front of me for what I can use my energy on. Most recently I've been spending it in Minecraft with my friend in VR. I will eventually move onto trying to actually make Unity games. I am enjoying writing stories, but I've only been writing for my creative writing class. It seems I've got a certain enjoyment for the craft.

You can contact me either on Discord: @neon64makoto, or via my main Email: neon64makoto@gmail.com
