About Me:

I'm Makoto Bennett. I am (at the time of writing this) 17 years old, my birthday is 4/12/2009. I spent a good portion of my life making creative maps in Fortnite Creative, from Chapter 1 Season 7 when it came out, all the way to Chapter 7 Season 4, when I stopped using Fortnite altogether.

Technically I've been coding since I was 10 or so because I started messing with Scratch. But when it comes to actually coding without the use of any AI, I have only recently started. Which is why this website has come to fruition. Before this I had a partially vibe coded website that I wasn't very happy with, and I knew I should make a website myself without the use of any AI. So that's what I did, and of course you are viewing that now.

I am currently still going through school, and when I graduate next year, I intend on going to a free coding bootcamp. I walk about a mile to and from school every school day, I enjoy the walk and get to listen to music. My main passions are coding, helping with technology, and creating. I also love music, but I do not create any. Now that I don't use Fortnite Creative anymore I have many options in front of me for what I can use my creative energy on. Most recently I've been spending it in Minecraft with my friend in VR. I will eventually move onto trying to actually make Unity games. I am enjoying writing stories, but I've only been writing for my creative writing class. It seems I've got a certain enjoyment for the craft.

I've now started making Unity projects, with my first one being the background of this very site now. I also am making the models myself using blender, which has been fairly fun so far. I then get free materials from Poly Haven and manually assign them to my models in Unity. I started out with a normal 3D URP, but eventually swapped it to HDRP to get the real time reflections.

My favorite games are: Portal 2, Doom Eternal, and Need For Speed: Heat. I have many more games I love, but those will always be the top 3. I am a part of the small discord mod team for Gunman Contracts: Stand Alone, and I of course LOVE Gunman Contracts the game. One of the best VR shooters I've had the pleasure of playing.

I exclusively played on my PS4 back when I was younger, I really only played Fortnite and NFS Heat. My house burned down mid covid around the start of 2022, while I was still in 6th grade. I then stayed at my great grand parents house for the rest of my 6th grade year playing on an XBOX one that barely functioned. When we finally scraped up enough donations and money, we bought a house from someone and now I live there. I started 7th grade at a new school and have been here ever since. I used my Fathers and Mothers PCs to play fortnite and NFS Heat after a while and then the holy grail of bugs and creation came out: Creative 2.0, AKA, UEFN. I quickly understood the Unreal Editor and started making UEFN maps. It wasn't until I ran into a lighting issue while making Flatline City when I found the Unreal Engine lights I could use. 1 map later and I had mastered using the UE Point, Spot, and Area lights. But then Epic Games started their shift into AI and their new AI vibecoded launcher was killing peoples PCs, so I finally decided it was time for me to stop using Fortnite and get into some real game developement. And that's where I'm at while I write this, Unity is great fun compared to the infinite load times of the Unreal Engine.
